# 📊 Business Performance Dashboard (Tableau)

## 📌 Overview

This project presents a Tableau dashboard analyzing retail business performance. It focuses on revenue, profitability, customer behavior, regional efficiency, and sales trends to identify key business insights and risks.

---

## 🎯 Business Problem

Organizations often struggle to understand:

* Where profit leakage is occurring
* Which customers contribute most to revenue (and risk dependency)
* How sales are trending over time
* Which regions are performing efficiently
* Whether revenue is actually translating into profit

This dashboard solves these problems through interactive visual analytics.

---

## 📊 Dashboard Components

### 1. Revenue vs Profit

Compares total revenue against profit to evaluate overall business performance and margin efficiency.

### 2. Profit Leakage

Highlights areas where high revenue does not translate into proportional profit, identifying inefficiencies.

### 3. Customer Concentration

Analyzes revenue dependency on key customers and highlights potential business risk from over-reliance.

### 4. Regional Profit Efficiency

Evaluates which regions are generating strong profit versus underperforming regions.

### 5. Monthly Sales Trend

Tracks sales performance over time to identify seasonal patterns and growth trends.
